Spiti in Snow: Christmas & New Year Expedition

In winter, Spiti turns white, quiet and magical, and few travellers make it here in December. You take the 3AC train from Bandra Terminus, loop up through Kinnaur to Chitkul, the last village on the Indo-Tibet road, spend two nights in Kaza for the high villages, then ring in 2027 in Kalpa facing the Kinner Kailash range and spend New Year's Day in Shimla.

Before you go

What to pack

  • Heavy down jacket and 2 sets of thermals
  • Woollen cap, gloves, socks and a muffler
  • Waterproof shoes with good grip
  • Sunglasses, SPF 50 sunscreen and lip balm
  • Power bank and a soft duffle bag
  • Personal medicines (ask your doctor about altitude)
  • Original government photo ID (Aadhaar or DL)
  • Some cash, since ATMs are rare in Spiti
Open the full packing checklist

Fitness

Mostly road days, but you sleep above 3,200 m for three nights and go up to 4,587 m at Komic. Walk 30–45 minutes a day for a few weeks before the trip, and tell us in advance about any medical condition.

Weather

−5 to −20°C. Snowfall, black ice or landslides can close roads at short notice, so the trip captain may change the order of the day for your safety.

Local etiquette

Walk clockwise around monasteries and stupas, ask before photographing people, never take fossils from Langza, and no smoking or alcohol inside the vehicle.

Questions about this trip

Which train do we take?

3rd AC from Bandra Terminus at 11:00 AM on 25 Dec, arriving Ambala Cantt at 8:47 AM on 26 Dec. The return leaves Ambala Cantt at 6:35 PM on 2 Jan and reaches Bandra Terminus at 4:10 PM on 3 Jan. Meals on the train are not included; order via IRCTC e-Catering or carry your own.

Is everything on the route guaranteed?

No. This is a December–January trip. Chitkul, Nako, Hatu Peak, Lingti and the Langza–Hikkim–Komic circuit depend on road and weather conditions. Reach times are approximate, and sightseeing missed due to weather, road or permission issues is not refunded.

What if snow blocks the road?

The trip captain and driver will reroute or reorder the day for safety. If the group has to stay an extra day, it costs ₹2,500 per person per day.

Are rooms heated?

Stays are chosen for warm rooms and snowy views, but some hotels charge extra for a room heater. Carry a hot water bottle; it is the single most useful thing at night.

Which phone network works?

Only BSNL and Jio work in most of Spiti.

How much do I pay upfront?

Your seat is confirmed once we receive the advance payment, and the balance is due before departure. The booking amount is non-refundable.

Are prices inclusive of GST?

No. Prices are shown before 5% GST, and international trips also attract TCS. Both are listed under 'Not included' on each itinerary.
