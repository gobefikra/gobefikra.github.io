Cancellation and refund policy

This policy applies to all trips booked on Book Befikar unless a trip page states different terms. Draft for review: confirm with your legal adviser before launch.

Last updated 11 October 2026

If you cancel

  • 30 days or more before departure: 25% of the package cost is deducted.
  • 15–29 days before departure: 50% is deducted.
  • 7–14 days before departure: 75% is deducted.
  • Less than 7 days before departure, or no-show: no refund.
  • The booking amount is non-refundable.

Train tickets

  • Train tickets are non-refundable once booked.
  • Any train refund follows IRCTC rules only.

If the trip is cancelled

  • If the trip is cancelled due to weather, natural calamity or government orders, you get a credit note after non-refundable costs are deducted.
  • No refunds for sightseeing missed due to weather, road or permission issues.

Booking

  • Your seat is confirmed after the advance payment; the balance is due before departure.
