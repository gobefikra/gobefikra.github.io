Spiti Valley in winter

Spiti sits in the rain shadow of the Himalaya, between Kinnaur and Ladakh. In winter it turns into a white desert: frozen lakes, monasteries above the clouds and villages above 4,000 m. The road via Shimla and Kinnaur stays open all year, which makes it India's classic winter road trip.

Altitude
3,800 m in Kaza, 4,587 m at Komic
Winter temperatures
−5 to −20°C
Best time
December to February for snow, June to September for summer
Gateway
Ambala Cantt, Shimla

Places you will stand in

All of these are on our winter route.

  • Key Monastery

    A thousand-year-old gompa stacked on a hill above the Spiti river. Butter tea with the monks is part of the visit.

  • Hikkim

    Home to one of the highest post offices in the world, at about 4,400 m. Send a postcard home.

  • Langza

    A golden Buddha watching over a village where the fields hold marine fossils from when this was a sea bed.

  • Chicham Bridge

    Asia's highest suspension bridge, hanging over a gorge between Kibber and Chicham.

  • Chitkul

    The last village on the Indo-Tibet road, once a stop on the old Hindustan-Tibet trade route.

  • Kalpa

    A Kinnauri village facing the Kinner Kailash range. We ring in the New Year here.
